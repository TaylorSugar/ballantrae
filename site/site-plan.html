Reading the map

Ballantrae Common leads in from Highway 48 to two traffic circles, First Round and Final Round, joined by The Masters South and The Masters North. Clusters of homes sit between the golf holes, with ponds and woodland around the edges.

Source: Maurice Smith street-naming article (2013) and the site plan above.
