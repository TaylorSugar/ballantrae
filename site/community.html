The community & lifestyle

What Ballantrae Golf and Country Club offers, who built it, how it is organized, and where the golf course fits in.

Where it is

Ballantrae Golf and Country Club is a master-planned adult lifestyle community in the hamlet of Ballantrae, Whitchurch-Stouffville, York Region. The main entrance road from Highway 48 is Ballantrae Common, and the property sits just north of Aurora Road.

Sources: Maurice Smith, Naming the Streets of Ballantrae G&CC (2013, from Sugar Team files); original builder site plan; Fairways Golf & Travel: Doing it well at Ballantrae.

How big it is

The original builder brochure describes roughly 900 homes. The condominium map in our files shows 894 units across five condominium corporations, plus a separate corporation for the recreation centre.

Sources: builder brochure (site plan); condominium map (Sugar Team files). The unit total is the sum of the five counts printed on that map.

The golf course

An 18-hole course at the heart of the community

  • Ballantrae Golf Club is a public 18-hole, par-72 course of 6,545 yards, with rental clubs, a driving range, lessons, a junior membership and a restaurant. (Golf Canada facility listing)
  • It was designed by Doug Carrick and opened in 2001. Fairways Golf & Travel describes generous fairways, raised and contoured greens and a walkable routing, with the homes kept on one side of the holes and now screened by mature trees. (Fairways Golf & Travel: Your Next Round, Ballantrae)
  • The club's own site lists a practice area with driving range, chipping and putting greens, plus a monitored pace of play. (Ballantrae Golf Club: 2026 rates)
Public course, not private. The course is open to the public, so ask us or the club how residents' play, tee times and any member programs work before you buy. Resident access terms: to confirm with the club

Recreation centre

Pool, fitness, billiards and a place to gather

The recreation centre's layout shows a swimming pool and whirlpool with locker rooms and a sauna, an exercise room, a billiards room, a card room, a library, a lounge, and party rooms with a kitchen. The original builder brochure also lists tennis courts and a hot tub among the homeowners' amenities.

The brochure map marks the gatehouse, clubhouse and restaurant, practice area, recreation centre, tennis, ponds and wooded natural trails. See the site plan and maps →

Sources: recreation centre layout and builder brochure (Sugar Team files). Hours, fees and rules are set by the condominium corporations; confirm current amenity details with the corporation.

How Ballantrae is organized

Five condominium corporations, named for golf's greats

Streets are named after champions. Each condominium corporation has its own theme, according to a street-naming article by resident Maurice Smith (Sept. 30, 2013), kept in the Sugar Team files.
